# Activities & analysis / Investigate sections and FIT

Retain exact activity IDs and selected section bounds. Time bounds are elapsed seconds; distance bounds are recorded kilometers. When several candidate climbs/laps match, inspect candidates and ask only if the intended selection remains ambiguous. Never substitute whole-ride or unrelated lap averages for a section. Discover unknown FIT fields/units before inspecting them. Query bounded streams and explain missing timestamp coverage. Repeated high-power efforts do not prove sprint intent.
