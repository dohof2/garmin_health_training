# Coaching, VO₂ sync and graph QC — 11 October 2026

Implemented a Training coaching brief and read-only assistant preview tool. Goals: FTP, endurance, race preparation and maintaining fitness; endurance/race sport can be cycling or running. The brief stores an optional confirmed FTP, 4–24 week horizon, race details and dated fixed commitments. Natural language can extract weekdays, ISO dates, duration, start time and effort; missing facts remain questions. Assistant follow-ups retain the brief and week independently of the text-history window. Save brief, create draft and accept plan are explicit local actions.

Weekly generation preserves fixed session timing/duration, subtracts existing sessions from confirmed volume, avoids overlaps, protects surrounding days for long/hard commitments and uses at most one added controlled cycling interval session. Hard fixed commitments occupy that quality slot. Endurance prioritizes the longest available slot. Race drafts approaching the event shorten generated sessions and protect freshness/recovery. Completed/published sessions cannot be replaced. Replacement retains cancelled workouts and revision history. Context fingerprints prevent creating drafts after preferences, workouts or the local day change. Feedback remains editable; coached timing/dose changes go through a new preview.

The longer-term roadmap is a qualitative direction, reassessed each week. It is not an automatically scheduled/progressively intensified 12-week program. The 2×6 minute interval dose, recovery buffers, phase lengths and 30-minute pre-event cap are provisional engineering defaults, not validated universal prescriptions. Relevant primary evidence for future refinement: [taper meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37163550/), [intensity distribution meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38717713/), [network meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39888556/). Saved feedback and the existing reviewed progression proposal remain available. Preferences/restrictions must be complete and fresh before calendar creation.

An unclassified generic fitness estimate is not relabelled as running. Line/bar builders also display their date axis. The assistant understands time-axis requests and refinements. ## Readiness and maintenance follow-up

Readiness v2 uses actual completed-sleep wake time as the morning observation cutoff, allowing early wake-ups; the configurable hour remains only a fallback when wake time is absent. Existing saved parameter choices migrate to a new version and prior stored calculations remain intact. Today's locally verified score became 68 once the unnecessary 08:00 gate was removed.

Added a distinct next-morning forecast API, dashboard dial, manual refresh and deterministic assistant route. Forecasts use recent median sleep/HRV/RHR plus known completed workout load. Synthetic inputs exist only in memory. Today's whole-day RHR is not treated as completed; future metrics and future workout ends are excluded. Forecasts assume no further exercise before the next morning.

The forecaster evaluates up to 100 past target days. It compares the median-input prototype forecast with ridge regression correcting its residuals, using physiological trends and workload features. Standardization and training use earlier examples only. Twenty initial training examples precede walk-forward evaluation; regression is selected only with at least fourteen evaluation mornings and over 5% lower MAE. Missing dates are excluded rather than filled. Historical checks reconstruct corrected records and cannot prove live forecast accuracy. The displayed error range is an empirical 80th-percentile absolute-error envelope (minimum ±5), not a calibrated confidence interval; selection uses the same check set. The target is the app score, not Garmin's proprietary score or recovery itself.
