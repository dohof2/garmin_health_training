# Nutrition conversation and targets — 21 October 2026

## Implemented

- Nutrition's dedicated assistant is the first card. It has its own conversation, food lookup and target calculator, separate from the training coach. Recent conversation, pending portion review and target proposals stay in the browser session across refreshes; target and food proposals are separate so changing topics does not discard a target proposal.
- Server context includes the selected local date, meal category, all current meal drafts, logged meals, known totals/remaining nutrients, saved targets and reusable-meal names. Pending food identities, portions, questions and meal assignments are validated and supplied to the next turn. Only public food names go to product search.
- Hebrew/English descriptions, corrections and follow-ups can contain breakfast, lunch, supper and snacks together or separately. Foods retain their meal category, and the editor keeps separate meal drafts. Confirming a portion or staging a draft does not log a meal.
- Yogurt `גביע` proposes 150 g with Israeli reference links and a confirmation question. Half/two/numeric containers are supported. Cottage cheese has a separate 250 g reference; an unspecified container is not universally converted to 150 g. Explicit gram corrections take priority.
- Bounded Open Food Facts lookup offers sourced product candidates; ordinary unbranded foods use offline USDA composition alternatives rather than banana-flavored products for a plain banana. Online failures and generic matches are explicit, missing nutrients remain unknown, and the user selects the actual food/preparation. Lookup uses verified TLS, request limits and bounded caching. FoodsDictionary blocked direct automated access during verification; it is linked as a portion reference, not falsely described as a working live scraper.
- Natural target requests produce structured goal/input requests. Arithmetic is performed by the backend calculator. Height comes from Garmin profile when available; latest dated weight, age/equation coefficient and 28 days of steps/completed activity inform the estimate. Missing inputs and target weight prompt a focused question.
- Maintenance uses complete Garmin expenditure days only when at least 14 usable days span all four weeks; otherwise a Mifflin resting estimate and an explicitly labeled activity heuristic are used. Today, duplicate source records and incomplete daily expenditure are excluded. Exercise is already included and is not added again.
- Conservative app defaults propose up to a 10%/300 kcal deficit for weight loss or 5%/250 kcal surplus for muscle gain. Protein uses 1.2–1.6 g/kg depending on activity/goal, fat starts at 25% of energy, and carbs use the remaining energy. These defaults are not a clinical prescription or a guaranteed rate of change. The review shows measurements, dates, activity window, maintenance method, macros, assumptions and sources.
- Adult-only and balanced-intake guards handle underage requests, low-weight loss goals, unusually low estimates, aggressive rates and declared clinical situations. Muscle gain is distinguished from scale-weight gain and linked to resistance training. No target is saved without the review action; saving recomputes the proposal and checks its fingerprint/revision. Existing logged days keep their target snapshot.

## Evidence and limitations

Live selected-assistant checks produced breakfast yogurt/bread and lunch rice/chicken from one Hebrew paragraph. Staging yogurt, then rice, then switching back retained the separate breakfast/lunch drafts. A separate follow-up added only a banana to lunch. [Meal evidence](separate-meals.png).

A live test target used recorded Garmin height, latest dated weight and four weeks of activity and produced a reviewable calorie/protein/fat/carb proposal. No personal meal or nutrition target was saved during QC. [Target proposal](target-proposal.png).

A later Hebrew confirmation retained 118 g banana and its lunch assignment. The proposed central portion is now a typed, range-validated field so the review button uses the same amount as the reply instead of substituting the midpoint of its uncertainty range. [Final conversation evidence](nutrition-final.png).

Open Food Facts initially returned HTTP 503 for some queries, later returned product matches, and exposed an irrelevant-flavored-product issue for unbranded bananas; generic basics now use USDA alternatives. Live service availability and community label completeness remain variable. Generic alternatives are not exact Israeli branded-product matches.

49 nutrition tests passed: existing logging/targets/photo/backup behavior plus conversation context, meal groups, portions/confirmations/corrections, product lookup/fallback, activity-derived targets, incomplete data, duplicates/current-day exclusion, safety guards and reviewed/stale saves. The earlier combined focused run passed 192 tests; two later pending-context regressions and a central-portion consistency regression also passed in the 49-test nutrition run. Frontend production build and whitespace checks passed.

## Sources used for the implementation

- [Israeli Ministry of Health food-group portions](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/guide/dietary-guidelines/he/files_publications_food_food-groups-reg.pdf) and [FoodsDictionary yogurt reference](https://www.foodsdictionary.co.il/Recipes/18635) support the yogurt portion proposal; actual products still need confirmation.
- [CDC gradual weight loss](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/losing-weight/index.html), [NIDDK adult planner scope](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/weight-management/body-weight-planner), [NIH sports nutrition](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ExerciseAndAthleticPerformance-HealthProfessional/), [National Academies macro ranges](https://www.nationalacademies.org/news/report-offers-new-eating-and-physical-activity-targets-to-reduce-chronic-disease-risk), [Mifflin equation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711/) and [energy-surplus trial](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40798-023-00651-y). The specific percentage adjustments, coverage thresholds, calorie floors and step/activity-factor cutoffs are conservative app heuristics, not quoted guideline prescriptions.
- [Open Food Facts API](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/ref-cheatsheet/) and the existing pinned USDA Foundation catalog supply food composition with attribution.
